Subject // Public Definition

VSI-PUB-ERELYT-01
THE
SIGNAL

Erelyt is not the website. The website is the institutional frame built around him. His voice, broadcasts, images, stories, and appearances are the signal; VSI is the organization that labels, packages, archives, and releases selected portions of that signal to the public. The resulting tension is deliberate: Erelyt can speak for himself, while VSI can only publish its approved interpretation of what it thinks it owns.
